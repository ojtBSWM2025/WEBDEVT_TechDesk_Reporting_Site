Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about IT reporting issues, tracking your reports, and our support process.

All Questions

How do I report an IT issue?

Go to Report an Issue, fill out the required details, add supporting evidence if available, confirm the information, and click Submit Report.

How can I track the status of my report?

Go to Track a Report and enter your reference number to check the latest status of your submitted report.

How long does it take to resolve a report?

The time needed depends on the type and severity of the reported IT issue. You can check your report status through the tracking page.

Can I edit or cancel my report?

If you need to update or cancel a submitted report, contact the IT support staff for assistance.

What IT issues can I report?

You can report campus Wi-Fi connection problems, laboratory hardware or software issues, projector and AV problems, login or account-related problems, and anything else under the Other category.

Who can use TechDesk?

Benilde students and employees can use TechDesk to report campus IT-related issues.

Who handles my submitted report?

The IT Admin or IT Support Staff reviews and handles submitted campus IT reports.

What if I lost my reference number?

Contact the IT support staff with your name and ID number so they can help you find your report.

Do I need an account to submit a report?

No separate account is needed. Provide your full name, Student/Employee ID, and email on the report form.

Can I report a login or email problem?

Yes. Choose Login / Account Issues as the category and describe what happens when you try to sign in.

What if my concern is not listed?

Choose Other as the category and describe the problem in as much detail as you can.

How do I contact IT support?

Use the contact details in the footer of the Home page.
