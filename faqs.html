Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about IT reporting issues, tracking your reports, and our support process.

All Questions

How do I report an IT issue?

Go to the Report an Issue page, fill out the required information, and submit your report.

How can I track my report?

Enter your reference number on the Track a Report page to check your report status.

What IT issues can I report?

You can report campus Wi-Fi connection problems and laboratory hardware or software issues.

Who can use TechDesk?

Benilde students and employees can use TechDesk to report campus IT-related issues.

Can I upload screenshots as evidence?

Yes. You can upload supported screenshots or files to help explain the IT issue.

Who handles my report?

The Benilde IT Admin or IT Support Staff reviews and handles submitted reports.

How long does it take to resolve a report?

Resolution time depends on the type and severity of the IT issue.
